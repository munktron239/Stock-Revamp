These blacklist / whitelist files tell Restock not to remove certain squad assets

This will let you use certain deprecated parts along side the models provided by Restock

I had considered only including these files in OldPartsRevived, but i figured i might as well include them here too

The 'no-restock-for-deprecated-parts' patch also contributes to this, telling restock to "ignore" some of the deprecated parts in-game

Here's a list:

highGainAntenna5
liquidEngine
liquidEngine2
spotLight1
spotlight2
smallRadialEngine
roverBody
Size2LFB
probeCoreHex

Normally, Restock would patch the parts to match their updated versions. But now, the old ones will have Ven's models.

So now you can have both Nertea's and Ven's models in-game!
